# Privacy Policy for Purple IPTV

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026

Purple IPTV ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Purple IPTV mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We **DO NOT** collect any personal data. Purple IPTV is designed to be a privacy-first media player.

- **Personal Data:** We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
- **Usage Data:** We do not track how you use the app, which channels you watch, or for how long.
- **Login Credentials:** The credentials you enter (URL, Username, Password) to access your IPTV service are stored **LOCALLY** on your device using secure storage. These credentials never leave your device and are never sent to our servers.

### 2. Content Disclaimer
Purple IPTV does not provide, host, or broadcast any media content. It is a tool (player) that allows users to stream their own content or playlists. We are not responsible for the content added by users.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Our application is built with Flutter. We do not use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase) or advertising networks that track your behavior.

### 4. Permissions
The app may request the following permissions only for functional purposes:
- **Internet:** Required to stream content from your provider.
- **Storage:** Required if you wish to record or download content locally.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
